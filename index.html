From a listing to a shipping quote

  1. Confirm the item

    Save the source listing URL and the selected variant. For clothing, compare the seller’s flat measurements with a garment you already own.

  2. Check agent support

    If you plan to use LoveGoBuy, first confirm it accepts the original seller link for your item. Check the seller’s return window and the agent’s return process.

  3. Review photos and parcel costs

    If warehouse QC photos are available, check the variant, labels and visible damage. Use your destination and the packed parcel’s weight and dimensions to get a shipping quote.

For a fair comparison, total the item price, domestic delivery, agent fees, international freight and any applicable taxes or duties in the same currency.

LoveGoBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Do I need Excel to use the spreadsheet?

The catalog works in your browser. Search by product name or source item ID, or use the category filter to narrow the list.

Can I paste a HipobuyFind link directly into LoveGoBuy?

Open the HipobuyFind listing and look for the original seller URL. Check that your agent accepts that URL and shows your chosen variant before paying.

Does the listed price include international shipping?

Each amount is a product-price reference from HipobuyFind. Ask your agent for a separate parcel quote using your destination and package measurements.

What should I check in warehouse QC photos?

Compare the size label, color, quantity and visible condition with your order. If a measurement matters to the fit, ask whether the agent can provide a photo with a measuring tape.
